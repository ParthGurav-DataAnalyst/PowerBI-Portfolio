# Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

Developed a comprehensive Sales Performance Analytics solution in Power BI to monitor revenue performance, sales targets, achievement metrics, customer contributions, and product-level sales trends. The dashboard provides management teams with actionable insights into sales performance and supports data-driven business decisions.

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales Performance & Target Achievement

Provides a high-level view of:

- Annual Sales Performance
- Monthly Sales Achievement
- Target vs Actual Analysis
- Sales Plan Monitoring
- Individual Sales Contributor Performance
- Achievement Percentage Tracking
- Current Month Sales Performance
- Daily Sales Monitoring

### 2. FY Sales Comparison

Provides detailed year-over-year sales analysis including:

- Financial Year Sales Comparison
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Top Customer Analysis
- Top Product Analysis
- Sales Contributor Comparison
- Revenue Performance Tracking
- Historical Sales Analysis
- Multi-Year Sales Reporting
